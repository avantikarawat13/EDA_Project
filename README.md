# Amazon Prime Video EDA 

## Project Overview

This project performs **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on the Amazon Prime Video dataset to uncover insights about content distribution, genres, ratings, release trends, countries of production, and other key characteristics available on the platform.

The objective is to understand the content library of Amazon Prime Video and identify patterns that can support business decisions and content strategy.

---

## Dataset Information

The dataset contains information about movies and TV shows available on Amazon Prime Video, including two csv files:
titles.csv
credits.csv

---

## Project Objectives

* Understand the distribution of Movies and TV Shows.
* Analyze content growth over the years.
* Identify the most common genres.
* Explore content ratings and certifications.
* Examine country-wise content production.
* Analyze movie durations and TV show seasons.
* Discover trends in content addition to the platform.

## Data Cleaning Steps

The following preprocessing steps were performed:

1. Handled missing values.
2. Removed duplicate records.
3. Converted date columns into datetime format.
4. Extracted year and month information.
5. Standardized categorical variables.
6. Cleaned duration and rating fields.

## Key Insights

* Movies constitute the majority of Amazon Prime Video's content library.
* Significant growth in content additions occurred in recent years.
* Drama and Comedy are among the most common genres.
* The United States contributes the highest number of titles.
* Most movies have a runtime between 80–120 minutes.
* TV Shows generally consist of 1–3 seasons.
